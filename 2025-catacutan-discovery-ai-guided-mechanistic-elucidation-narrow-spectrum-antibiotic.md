# Paper Summary

### Authors
- Denise B. Catacutan et al.

### Journal
- Nature Microbiology

### Publication Date
- 2025

### DOI
- https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-025-02142-0

## Keywords
- narrow-spectrum antibiotic
- Enterobacteriaceae
- AIEC
- deep learning
- LolCDE

## Main Idea
- The paper discovers enterololin, a narrow-spectrum Enterobacteriaceae-active antibiotic candidate, and uses AI-guided analysis to elucidate mechanism.

## Evidence Supporting the Main Idea
- Screened 10,747 bioactive small molecules against AIEC.
- Enterololin activity improves with subinhibitory SPR741 for outer-membrane permeabilization.
- Resistant mutants map mainly to `lolC`/`lolE`, supporting LolCDE-linked mechanism.
- In vitro resistance frequency reported at about 10^-8 to 10^-7.
- HEK293 cytotoxicity IC50 around 100 ug ml^-1 and efficacy shown in mouse AIEC infection models.

## Main Novelty
- Combines phenotypic discovery with molecular substructure/deep-learning-guided mechanism inference for a microbiome-sparing antibiotic.

## Datasets Used for Evaluation
- 10,747-compound antibacterial screening dataset.
- Clinical Enterobacteriaceae isolates and evolved resistant mutants.
- Mouse infection experiments for AIEC suppression.

## Experimental Procedure
- High-throughput antibacterial screening.
- Combination testing with SPR741.
- Resistance mapping via lab evolution and mutation analysis.
- AI-guided molecular mechanism interrogation.
- Cytotoxicity and in vivo efficacy testing.

## Key Biology Insights
- Targeted perturbation of lipoprotein trafficking can provide Enterobacteriaceae-selective antibacterial activity.

## Implications
- Offers a path to narrower-spectrum therapeutics that reduce collateral microbiome disruption.
